# Involute Spur Gears

<link-summary>Formulae and engineering requirements for standard, profile shifted and internal involute spur gears.</link-summary>

A spur gear has teeth parallel to the axis. The profile is an involute of the base circle, generated by a rack
with the tooth proportions $h_a = m$, $h_f = m + c$ and a standard clearance $c = 0.25\,m$.
The formulae below hold for external gears and for internal (ring) gears. A helix angle of zero in
[Helical Gears](Helical-Gears.md) gives exactly these results.

## Standard external spur gear

Profile shift $x = 0$.

| No | Item | Symbol | Formula | Pinion | Gear |
|----|------|--------|---------|--------|------|
| 1 | Module | $m$ | - | 2.5 | 2.5 |
| 2 | Pressure angle | $\alpha$ | - | 20° | 20° |
| 3 | Number of teeth | $z_1, z_2$ | - | 18 | 25 |
| 4 | Centre distance | $a$ | $\dfrac{m (z_1 + z_2)}{2}$ | 53.750 | |
| 5 | Pitch diameter | $d$ | $m \cdot z$ | 45.000 | 62.500 |
| 6 | Base diameter | $d_b$ | $d \cos\alpha$ | 42.286 | 58.731 |
| 7 | Addendum | $h_a$ | $m$ | 2.500 | 2.500 |
| 8 | Dedendum | $h_f$ | $1.25\,m$ | 3.125 | 3.125 |
| 9 | Whole depth | $h$ | $h_a + h_f$ | 5.625 | 5.625 |
| 10 | Outside diameter | $d_a$ | $d + 2 h_a$ | 50.000 | 67.500 |
| 11 | Root diameter | $d_f$ | $d - 2 h_f$ | 38.750 | 56.250 |
| 12 | Circular pitch | $p$ | $\pi m$ | 7.854 | 7.854 |
| 13 | Base pitch | $p_b$ | $\pi m \cos\alpha$ | 7.380 | 7.380 |
| 14 | Circular tooth thickness | $s$ | $\dfrac{\pi m}{2}$ | 3.927 | 3.927 |

## Profile shifted external spur gear

A profile shift $x\,m$ moves the generating rack away from the gear centre (positive $x$) or towards it
(negative $x$). It changes the tooth thickness, the tip and root diameters and the centre distance of the pair, but
it does **not** change the pitch diameter or the base diameter.

| No | Item | Symbol | Formula | Pinion | Gear |
|----|------|--------|---------|--------|------|
| 1 | Module | $m$ | - | 2.5 | 2.5 |
| 2 | Pressure angle | $\alpha$ | - | 20° | 20° |
| 3 | Number of teeth | $z$ | - | 18 | 25 |
| 4 | Profile shift coefficient | $x$ | - | 0.4 | 0.2 |
| 5 | Bottom clearance | $c$ | $0.25\,m$ | 0.625 | 0.625 |
| 6 | Pitch diameter | $d$ | $m z$ | 45.000 | 62.500 |
| 7 | Base diameter | $d_b$ | $d \cos\alpha$ | 42.286 | 58.731 |
| 8 | Addendum | $h_a$ | $m (1 + x)$ | 3.500 | 3.000 |
| 9 | Dedendum | $h_f$ | $m (1 - x) + c$ | 2.125 | 2.625 |
| 10 | Whole depth | $h$ | $h_a + h_f$ | 5.625 | 5.625 |
| 11 | Outside diameter | $d_a$ | $d + 2 h_a$ | 52.000 | 68.500 |
| 12 | Root diameter | $d_f$ | $d - 2 h_f$ | 40.750 | 57.250 |
| 13 | Circular tooth thickness | $s$ | $m \left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2 x \tan\alpha\right) - j_t$ | 4.655 | 4.291 |

The working centre distance, working pressure angle and contact ratio of this pair
(total shift $x_1 + x_2 = 0.6$) are worked out in [Gear Pair Calculations](Gear-Pair-Calculations.md).

<note>
The whole depth is independent of the profile shift: $h = 2 m + c$. Only the split between addendum and
dedendum changes.
</note>

## Internal spur gear

An internal gear has its teeth on the inside of a ring. The addendum points towards the centre, so the signs of
the addendum, dedendum and diameters reverse.

| No | Item | Symbol | Formula |
|----|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Pitch diameter | $d$ | $m z$ |
| 2 | Base diameter | $d_b$ | $d \cos\alpha$ |
| 3 | Addendum | $h_a$ | $m (1 - x)$ |
| 4 | Dedendum | $h_f$ | $m (1 + x) + c$ |
| 5 | Inside (tip) diameter | $d_a$ | $d - 2 h_a$ |
| 6 | Root diameter | $d_f$ | $d + 2 h_f$ |
| 7 | Circular tooth thickness (space width) | $s$ | $m \left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2 x \tan\alpha\right) + j_t$ for the tooth space |
| 8 | Rim thickness | $s_{rim}$ | given by the designer |

## Involute function

The involute function converts a pressure angle into a roll angle and is used in every shifted-gear calculation:

$$
\mathrm{inv}\,\alpha = \tan\alpha - \alpha \qquad (\alpha \text{ in radians})
$$

## Engineering requirements

* **Undercut.** A tooth is undercut at the root when the number of teeth is below
  $z_{min} = \dfrac{2}{\sin^2\alpha}$ (17 teeth for 20°). A profile shift of at least
  $x_{min} = 1 - \dfrac{z \sin^2\alpha}{2}$ avoids undercut on a gear with fewer teeth.
* **Pointed teeth.** Increasing $x$ makes the tooth thinner at the tip. A tip thickness below about $0.25\,m$
  to $0.4\,m$ is brittle; keep the tip diameter small enough to leave a land.
* **Interference.** The addendum of one gear must not run into the root fillet of the mate. For internal gears the
  tooth difference $z_2 - z_1$ must be large enough that the pinion tips do not touch the ring teeth when the
  gears are assembled (trochoidal interference). Use at least 10 teeth of difference as a first check.
* **Contact ratio.** A pair must keep $\varepsilon_\alpha \ge 1.2$; see [Gear Pair Calculations](Gear-Pair-Calculations.md).
* **Face width.** A face width of 6 to 12 modules is usual for spur gears. Wider faces need more accurate shaft
  alignment because the load concentrates at one end.
* **Standard module.** Prefer the ISO 54 preferred modules (1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 ...) so
  standard cutters can be used.
* **Rim thickness of an internal gear.** Keep the rim thickness at least $1.2\,h$ (about $2.5\,m$ to $3\,m$) to avoid
  rim deformation under load.

## Limits enforced by Gears

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Number of teeth | at least 1 |
| Module | greater than 0 |
| Face width | greater than 0 |
| Pressure angle | greater than 0° and less than 90° |
| Bottom clearance | defaults to $0.25\,m$ |
| Backlash | defaults to 0 |
